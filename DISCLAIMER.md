# Warning and disclaimer

**Read this before you use anything in this toolkit.**

## This can permanently brick your device

**Following this process could very well fully brick your device, leaving it permanently unusable.** That's a real possibility, not a formality. The device this toolkit came from got stuck during development, and was only recovered after days of reverse engineering. A similar failure on your device may have no recovery at all.

The process can also:
- **Destroy your data**, and data that can't be recreated, such as per-device keys, serial numbers, MAC addresses and DRM licences.
- **Void your warranty**, and break features such as certification, DRM or streaming quality for good.
- **Leave the device less secure.** It involves open debugging access, permissive security settings, root and closed-source binaries.
- **Cause damage through hardware work**, such as wiring a UART adapter or using the wrong voltage.

The safety rules, backups and checks here reduce the risk. **They don't remove it.** They've been tested on only one device, and many of the tools have had limited testing: the README lists which.

**Only use this on a device you own, and can afford to lose completely.**
